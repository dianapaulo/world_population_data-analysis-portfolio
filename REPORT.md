# 🌍 World Population Data Analysis (1960–2023)

**Author:** Diana Paulo 

**Date:** October 2025  

**Data Source:** [World Population Dataset](https://data360.worldbank.org/en/indicator/WB_HNP_SP_POP_TOTL?view=datatable) 

**Tools:** MYSQL, Excel, Power BI, Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook  

---

## 🧭 Overview
This report analyzes global population trends from **1960 to 2023**, focusing on:
- Population growth by country
- Urbanization and gender balance
- Age group distributions
- Ranking changes among nations
- Growth rate patterns

---

## 📊 1. Most Populous Countries

### **Top 10 (1960)**
China, India, United States, Rwanda, Japan, Indonesia, Brazil, Germany, United Kingdom, Bangladesh

### **Top 10 (2023)**
India, China, United States, Indonesia, Pakistan, Nigeria, Brazil, Bangladesh, Russia, Mexico

**Insight:**  
Six countries — **China, India, U.S., Indonesia, Brazil, and Bangladesh** — have remained in the top 10 for over **63 years**.

---

## 🧍‍♂️ 2. Least Populous Countries

### **1960**
Slovak Republic, St. Martin, Netherlands, Tuvalu, Andorra, Panama, Mauritius, São Tomé and Príncipe, Luxembourg, American Samoa

### **2023**
Tuvalu, Nauru, Palau, St. Martin, Gibraltar, San Marino, Monaco, Liechtenstein, Sint Maarten, Marshall Islands

**Insight:**  
Only **St. Martin** and **Tuvalu** remained among the least populous.  
The **Netherlands** moved from one of the smallest populations (1960) to rank **#69** in 2023.

## 👶 7. Age Distribution

| Age Group | 1960 | 2023 | Change |
|------------|------|------|--------|
| 0–14 years | 36.91% | 25.03% | ↓ Decreased |
| 15–64 years | 57.77% | 64.95% | ↑ Increased |
| 65+ years | 5.33% | 10.02% | ↑ Increased |

**Insight:**  
The global population is **aging** — fewer children and more elderly.

---

## 👩‍🦰 8. Gender & Age Breakdown (2023)

**Female:**
- 15 years & below → 970,155,559 (**12.14%**)  
- 15–64 years → 2,558,507,757 (**32.02%**)  
- 65+ years → 445,888,775 (**5.58%**)  

**Male:**
- 15 years & below → 1,029,872,096 (**12.89%**)  
- 15–64 years → 2,630,714,782 (**32.93%**)  
- 65+ years → 354,757,808 (**4.44%**)  

**Insight:**  
Males dominate in younger and working-age groups, while females lead in the elderly population.

## 🧠 **Summary of Key Insights**
- Global population grew by **~5 billion** people since 1960.

- Developing nations lead in population growth.
